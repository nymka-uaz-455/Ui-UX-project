# FocusStudy – Homework 1: Problem Definition and Initial Concept

## What Did I Complete This Week?

This week, I worked on defining the initial problem and concept for my UI/UX project, **FocusStudy**, a study-planning application designed for university students.

I created a FigJam board and organized it into five sections:

1. **Problem** – Defined the problem using the user, situation, goal, obstacle, and consequence framework.
2. **User and Situation** – Identified university students managing multiple courses, assignments, and deadlines as the target users.
3. **Goals and Constraints** – Defined the user's goal, the product's organizational goal, and the main constraints.
4. **Success Criterion** – Established a measurable criterion for evaluating the proposed solution.
5. **Assumptions and Open Questions** – Identified important assumptions, their sources, and the riskiest assumption requiring further research.

I also connected the key ideas with arrows and prepared a short pitch describing the problem and the next research objective.

## Essence of the Solution

FocusStudy aims to help university students organize their academic responsibilities, prioritize study tasks, and create realistic study schedules.

The initial concept focuses on reducing difficulties with study planning and helping students decide what to work on next. The proposed solution will be developed and refined based on user research and usability testing.
